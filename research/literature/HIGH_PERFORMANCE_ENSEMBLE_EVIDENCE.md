# Focused evidence update: strong heterogeneous ensembles

Reviewed3 October2026. Primary-source checks supplement the existing review and SciSpace screening; workbook/extracted rows unchanged. Results below are author-reported, not reproduced. Methods motivate hypotheses; no pooled accuracy leaderboard.

1. [Sravani & Koppu2026, Frontiers in Public Health](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/public-health/articles/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1847649/full), DOI10.3389/fpubh.2026.1847649. Sections3.3/3.5–3.7, Tables5/6/9: heterogeneous DenseNet201/EfficientNet-B3/ResNet101, end-to-end ImageNet fine-tuning,224px, focal loss/training augmentation and weighted probabilities. Reported DenseNet94.92%, B3 95.38%, ensemble96.37%; reported F1 is weighted, not our macro-F1. The text describes8012/2003 lesion-grouped partition but computes metrics on a1103-image unduplicated validation subset, unlike our1503image cohort. Its50-epoch/seed10/custom-head/normalization/optimizer package differs. Supports dense/scaled families and bounded global weighting; not a prediction that we will match its numbers.
2. [DermAI1.0, Diagnostics2023](https://www.mdpi.com/2075-4418/13/19/3159); [indexed primary full-text excerpt](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10573070/); DOI10.3390/diagnostics13193159. Indexed Table1/Section4.1 report DenseNet20195.18%, EfficientNet-B397.01% on an augmented HAM10000/K10 evaluation. Fulltext browser access was blocked in this review, so protocol details beyond the retrieved excerpt/PDF Section3.6 remain unresolved; no lesion-independent equivalence claimed. Supports candidate prioritization, not adopting reported accuracy/F1 as our expected result.
3. Existing SciSpace row4 SynthraXCoreNet (DenseNet201 plus residual/Xception/NASNet soft voting) and row13 deep-feature/ML ensembles remain extraction-only, not newly verified success evidence. Existing row3 ML-IGIA primary check supports constrained attention/fusion, with previously documented class-count/weight-fitting ambiguity. Base paper and HAM10000 authorities remain included.
4. [MedFusionNet primary paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-31816-2), already row12 in the workbook, supports CNN/ViT feature-fusion context. This is not evidence for an identical standalone Swin-T score on our protocol. Row32 ConvNeXt-Swin/AFF remains extraction-only for exact dataset/protocol here. Swin-T is a conditional architecture-diversity slot, not a promised HAM10000 champion.

**Decision:** two committed candidates in the plan: DenseNet201 next, then B3 subject to evidence/approval; conditional third Swin-T. Dense concatenation supplies a representation family missing from our current ConvNeXt/B0/V2 ensemble. B4/ResNet101 are reserve alternatives, not extra automatic runs. Retain our train-only class-weighted CE for fair screening; historical focal/strong-augmentation regressions do not support changing every new model at once. Equal voting first, then at most two additional global weighting templates on one selected member set, all development selection disclosed.
