# Attention and improvement evidence

This folder is a navigation entry point. Actual metrics/figures remain in their scientific packages.

- [Matched S03 versus S05 CBAM comparison](../model_comparison/structured/s03_s05_cbam_ablation/): EfficientNet-B0 versus B0 + CBAM on the same exploratory protocol/recipe. Accuracy tied; macro-F1 and class behaviour were mixed. CBAM did not universally improve performance.
- [S05 closeout](../../research/phase3/S05_CLOSEOUT.md): interpretation of the controlled attention comparison.
- [S79 longer ConvNeXt-Tiny + CBAM package](../final_cbam_development/v1/s79_convnext_tiny_cbam_exploratory_seed42/): later attention training and saved curves/metrics. Its recipe differs from S06, so their difference is not a pure CBAM effect.
- [Later bounded studies](../short_screening/): retained positive and negative augmentation, inference, feature/head, metadata and fusion evidence. The registry/closeouts establish which studies are comparable.

The [reporting evidence index](../../reporting/evidence/INDEX.md) groups comparisons by research question. Negative results may be summarised or placed in an appendix; historical scores and provenance remain unchanged.
